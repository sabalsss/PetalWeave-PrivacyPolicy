Help for Petal Weave: Color Sort on iPhone

Need help?

Email sabaldev670@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the game version (Settings in the game shows it at the bottom). We usually reply within a few days.

How do I play?

Drag the bottom flower of a vine, together with any matching flowers right above it, onto a vine that ends in the same kind of flower or onto an empty vine. Fill a vine with one kind of flower to make a bouquet. Collect every bouquet to finish the level. You can replay the guided lesson any time from Settings → How to Play.

I'm stuck on a level

Use Undo, Hint or Extra Vine in the bar at the bottom of the board, or tap Restart. Every level is checked to be solvable. Boosters can be bought with coins you earn by finishing levels; the game has no real-money purchases.

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone only. There are no accounts or cloud saves, so deleting the game deletes your progress. Your progress is included in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backups.

Sound, nature sounds and vibration

Open Settings in the game (Home → the gear button) to switch Sound Effects, Nature Sounds and Haptics on or off. The pause screen has quick switches too. The game follows your iPhone's silent switch.

Daily reminder

Turn the daily reminder on or off and choose its time in Settings → Daily Reminder. If notifications for Petal Weave are off in iOS Settings → Notifications, turn them on there first.

Privacy

Optional gameplay stats and crash reports are off unless you turn them on in Settings → Privacy & Terms. Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
