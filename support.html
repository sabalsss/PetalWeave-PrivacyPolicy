Help for Petal Weave: Color Sort on iPhone

Welcome! Thanks for playing Petal Weave. If something isn’t working, a level has you stuck, or you just want to share an idea, we’d love to hear from you.

Email Support sabaldev670@gmail.com

Report a bug

If the game crashes, freezes or shows something that looks wrong, please email us with:

  1. Your iPhone model and iOS version (iOS Settings → General → About).
  2. The game version, shown at the bottom of the game’s Settings screen.
  3. What you were doing when it happened, and the level number if you were playing.
  4. A screenshot or screen recording, if you can.

Gameplay help

Drag the bottom flower of a vine, together with any matching flowers right above it, onto a vine that ends in the same flower or onto an empty vine. Fill a vine with one kind of flower to make a bouquet, and collect every bouquet to finish the level.

Stuck? Try Undo, Hint or Extra Vine at the bottom of the board, or tap Restart. Every level is checked to be solvable. You can replay the lesson any time from Settings → How to Play.

If a level still seems impossible, email us the level number and we’ll take a look.

Send feedback

Ideas, favourite levels, things you’d like changed: all feedback is welcome. Send it to sabaldev670@gmail.com. We read every message and usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved? On your iPhone only. There are no accounts or cloud saves, so deleting the game deletes your progress. It is included in your iPhone’s own iCloud or computer backups.

How do I turn off sound or vibration? Open Settings in the game (the gear button on Home) and switch Sound Effects, Nature Sounds or Haptics off. The game also follows your iPhone’s silent switch.

How do I change the daily reminder? Use Settings → Daily Reminder in the game. If notifications are off for Petal Weave in iOS Settings → Notifications, turn them on there first.

Privacy

Optional gameplay stats and crash reports are off unless you turn them on in the game’s Settings → Privacy & Terms. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
