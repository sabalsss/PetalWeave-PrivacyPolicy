Petal Weave Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026 · Last updated: 8 November 2026

In short
  • No account, no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • Your game progress stays on your iPhone.
  • Anonymous gameplay analytics and crash reports are optional. They are only collected if you choose Allow & Continue or turn them on in Settings, and you can turn them off at any time.

1. Who we are

Petal Weave: Color Sort (“Petal Weave”, “the game”) is a free puzzle game for iPhone developed and operated by Sabal Chaulagain (“we”, “us”).

Questions, requests and data-deletion requests: sabaldev670@gmail.com.

2. Information stored only on your device

To save your game, Petal Weave keeps a file on your iPhone containing your progress and preferences: levels played and completed, the level in progress (so you can resume it), coins and seeds, boosters, your garden, cosmetics, achievements, daily-puzzle streaks, and settings such as sound, music, haptics, spoken praise, reminders and your privacy choices.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you delete the app. Like other app data, it may be included in backups of your iPhone that you make with Apple’s iCloud or a computer; those backups are controlled by you and Apple.

3. Optional gameplay analytics (Google Firebase Analytics)

Only if you choose Allow & Continue or turn on Share Gameplay Analytics in Settings, the game sends anonymous gameplay events to Google Firebase Analytics so we can see which levels are too hard or too easy and how features are used. Until then, analytics collection is switched off in the app.

What is sent

  • Gameplay events: tutorial started and completed; level started, won, got stuck, restarted or left; daily puzzle completed; boosters used (Undo, Hint, Extra Vine); garden opened; in-game coins or seeds spent and on which in-game item; screens viewed; the game resuming a saved level.
  • Event details: level number, number of moves, active play time, number of boosters and restarts, rejected moves, whether a level was won, and the game version.
  • Information Firebase adds automatically: a random app-instance identifier for this installation, the app version, device model, iOS version, language and region settings, session start and engagement time, first open and app updates, and an approximate location (country or region) that Google derives from your network’s IP address.

What is not collected

Your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA), or anything you type. The game uses a version of Firebase Analytics that does not include advertising-identifier support, and advertising and ad-personalisation features are disabled.

4. Optional crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics)

Only if you choose Allow & Continue or turn on Send Crash Reports in Settings, the game sends a report to Google Firebase Crashlytics when it crashes. A report contains the technical crash log (where in the code the crash happened), device model, iOS version, app version, free memory and disk space, a random installation identifier, and the level you were playing.

Crash reports created before you turned crash reporting on are deleted, not sent.

5. Identifiers and diagnostic information

When analytics or crash reporting is on, Firebase uses random identifiers generated for this installation of the game (an app-instance ID and a Firebase installation ID). They are not linked to your name or Apple Account, are not shared with other apps, and change if you reinstall the game. Turning analytics off resets the analytics identifier on your device.

6. Daily reminder notifications

After the tutorial, the game may ask whether you would like a daily reminder. If you agree, iOS asks for permission to show notifications. The reminder is a local notification scheduled on your iPhone for 7 pm each day; it sends no information to us or to anyone else, and the game does not use push notifications. You can turn the reminder off in the game’s Settings, or turn notifications off in iOS Settings → Notifications → Petal Weave.

7. Why we use information

  • To keep your game progress and settings on your device.
  • If you allow it: to understand how players progress, tune level difficulty, and improve features (analytics).
  • If you allow it: to find and fix crashes (crash reports).

We do not use information for advertising, profiling, or selling, and we do not combine it with data from other sources. Where the law requires a legal basis (for example in the EU/EEA and UK), optional analytics and crash reporting rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

8. Google Firebase

Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics are provided by Google LLC, which processes this information on our behalf as a service provider. Google’s information about how Firebase handles data: firebase.google.com/support/privacy, and Google’s Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

9. Sharing

We do not sell or rent information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Optional analytics and crash data are shared only with Google, as our service provider described above. We may disclose information if required by law.

11. Your choices

  • First launch: choose Allow & Continue to turn on optional analytics and crash reports, or Not Now to keep them off. Reading this policy does not turn anything on.
  • Any time: in the game, open Pause → Home → Settings and switch Share Gameplay Analytics and Send Crash Reports on or off independently. Turning analytics off stops collection immediately and resets the identifier; turning crash reports off deletes reports not yet sent.
  • The game works the same whether these are on or off.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your information, or to complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us at the address above to exercise them.

12. Children

Petal Weave is a puzzle game suitable for all ages and contains no advertising, chat, or purchases. It does not ask for personal information. Optional analytics and crash reporting stay off unless switched on, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has turned these on, you can switch them off in Settings and contact us to delete data already sent.

13. International processing

Google may process optional analytics and crash data in the United States and other countries where it or its subprocessors operate. Google states the safeguards it uses for international transfers in the Firebase privacy information linked above.

14. Changes to this policy

If the game’s data practices change, we will update this page and its “Last updated” date. If a change would collect new kinds of information, we will ask for your consent in the game where required.
