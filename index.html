Petal Weave Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026 · Last updated: 8 November 2026

The short version
  • Petal Weave has no accounts, no ads and no in-app purchases.
  • Your game progress is saved on your iPhone, not on our servers.
  • Sharing gameplay stats and crash reports with Google Firebase is optional and off until you turn it on in the game’s Settings → Privacy & Terms. Accepting our Terms of Service doesn’t turn it on, and playing never requires it.
  • If you do turn it on, it doesn’t include your name or email, it’s linked to a random ID rather than to you, and it’s never used for ads or tracking. You can turn it off again at any time and the game works exactly the same.

Who we are

Petal Weave: Color Sort (“Petal Weave” or “the game”) is a free puzzle game for iPhone made by Sabal Chaulagain (“we” or “us”).

If you have a question about privacy or want to make a request, email sabaldev670@gmail.com.

Your game progress stays on your iPhone

To remember where you are, the game saves a file on your iPhone with your levels, the level you’re in the middle of, coins, seeds, boosters, garden, decorations, achievements, daily-puzzle streak, the version of the Terms you accepted and when, and settings (such as sound, music, reminder time and your privacy choices).

We don’t receive this file. It’s removed when you delete the game. Like other app data, it can be included in iPhone backups you make with iCloud or a computer, which you and Apple control.

When you first open the game

The first time you open Petal Weave, a welcome screen asks you to read our Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy, and to tap Accept & Continue. The game records which version of the Terms you accepted and when, on your iPhone only, so it only asks again if the Terms change in an important way.

Accepting is not consent to gameplay stats or crash reports. Both stay off until you choose to turn them on in the game’s privacy settings (Pause → Home → Settings → Privacy & Terms). Each switch explains what it shares and asks you to tap Allow before it turns on. Leaving them off doesn’t change or limit the game in any way, and while both are off the Firebase software in the game isn’t started at all.

Gameplay stats (Google Firebase Analytics)

While Share Gameplay Analytics is on, the game sends information about how the game is played, so we can see which levels feel too hard or too easy and which features people enjoy.

What it includes

  • What happens in the game: starting and finishing the tutorial; starting, winning, getting stuck in, restarting or leaving a level; finishing the daily puzzle; using a booster; opening your garden; spending in-game coins or seeds (and on what); which screens you visit; and returning to a level you left unfinished.
  • Details about a level: the level number, how many moves you made, how long you actively played, how many boosters and restarts you used, how many moves didn’t fit, whether you won, and the version of the game.
  • Information Firebase adds: a random ID for your copy of the game, your device model, iOS version, language and region settings, when you started playing and for how long, when the game was first opened or updated, and an approximate location (such as country or city) that Google works out from your internet connection’s IP address.

What it doesn’t include

Your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, Apple’s advertising identifier, or anything you type. We use a version of Firebase Analytics without advertising-identifier support, and its advertising features are turned off.

Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics)

While Send Crash Reports is on, if the game crashes it sends a report so we can find and fix the problem. A report includes technical details about where the crash happened, your device model, iOS version, game version, available memory and storage, a random ID for your copy of the game, and the level you were playing.

If crash reporting is off, crash reports aren’t sent, and any saved from that time are deleted rather than sent later.

About the random IDs

Firebase uses random IDs created for your copy of the game so it can tell one player’s stats apart from another’s. They aren’t connected to your name or Apple Account, aren’t shared with other apps, and change if you reinstall the game. Turning gameplay stats off also resets the ID on your iPhone.

Daily reminders

After you finish the first tutorial level, the game asks once whether you’d like a gentle daily reminder. Only if you tap Enable Daily Reminders does iOS ask for permission to show notifications. The reminder is a local notification scheduled on your iPhone, at 7:00 PM by default; it doesn’t send any information to us or anyone else, and no server is involved.

You can change the reminder time or switch the reminder off at any time in the game’s Settings, or turn off notifications for Petal Weave in iOS Settings → Notifications. If you choose Not Now, the game won’t ask again; you can still switch the reminder on in Settings.

How we use information

  • To save your progress and settings on your iPhone.
  • To understand how players move through the game and to adjust level difficulty and features (gameplay stats).
  • To find and fix crashes (crash reports).

We don’t use any of this for advertising, we don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites, we don’t sell it, and we don’t combine it with information from other sources. Where the law asks for a legal basis (for example in the EU, EEA and UK), gameplay stats and crash reports are based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings.

Who we share information with

Gameplay stats and crash reports are processed for us by Google LLC, which provides Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. Google handles this information according to its own terms and privacy commitments: Firebase privacy information and Google Privacy Policy.

We don’t share information with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Google may process this information in the United States and other countries where it or its partners operate. Google describes the safeguards it uses for international transfers in the Firebase privacy information above.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Turn sharing off: open Settings → Privacy & Terms in the game and switch off Share Gameplay Analytics, Send Crash Reports, or both. Collection stops straight away. Turning gameplay stats off also resets the random ID; turning crash reports off deletes reports that haven’t been sent yet.
  • Delete stats already sent: in Settings → Privacy & Terms, copy your Analytics ID (under What’s collected & how to delete it), and email it to sabaldev670@gmail.com asking us to delete it. We’ll delete the stats linked to that ID using Google Analytics’ deletion tools.
  • Delete everything on your iPhone: delete the game.
  • Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to see, correct or delete information about you, to object to or limit how it’s used, or to complain to your local data-protection authority. Email us and we’ll help.

Children

Petal Weave is a gentle puzzle game suitable for all ages. It has no ads, chat or purchases, and it never asks for a name, email or other contact details. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. If you’re a parent or guardian, you can switch off gameplay stats and crash reports in Settings and email us to delete any stats already sent.

Changes to this policy

If the way the game handles information changes, we’ll update this page and the “Last updated” date above. If a change means collecting a new kind of information, we’ll ask in the game first where the law requires it.
